NEXUS TRAILING QUICK GUIDE
==========================

DEFAULT LADDER EXAMPLE
----------------------
Signal:
  Entry = 4400
  SL    = 4390
  TP1   = 4410
  TP2   = 4420
  TP3   = 4430

Default profile: NEXUS Ladder 50 + BE

At TP1:
  - Close 50% of INITIAL volume.
  - Move SL to Entry = 4400.

At TP2:
  - No additional close by default.
  - Move SL to TP1 = 4410.

At TP3:
  - TP3 is the hard final broker TP by default, so the remaining position closes.

If you configure TP4 instead:
  TP1 -> 50% close + SL Entry
  TP2 -> SL TP1
  TP3 -> SL TP2
  TP4 -> hard final broker TP

CLIENT POLICY PRIORITY
----------------------
Signal snapshot / admin policy always controls what a customer may run.
A future client may customize only when:
  1. subscription is active,
  2. trailing is enabled by Admin,
  3. assigned/allowed profile is active,
  4. allow_user_customize is enabled by Admin.
